Regular chats

Can Televo read them? Technically, yes.

They're encrypted on our servers with a key we manage — the same model as Telegram's cloud chats. It's what lets your history follow you to a new phone and makes group chats easy.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Synced live across all your devices
  • Groups, media, voice messages
  • Never used for ads or sold

Secret Chats

Can Televo read them? No.

They're end-to-end encrypted: the keys are made on your phone and never leave it. Our servers carry the message without being able to open it.

  • Hybrid post-quantum key exchange
  • Safety numbers you can compare
  • Warns you and pauses sending if their key changes
  • History stays on the device: reinstall, and old messages can't be opened

Inside a Secret Chat.

Standard, published cryptography — nothing invented in-house. If you want to check our work, every step below is in the source code.

  1. Your phone makes the keys

    An X25519 key pair and an ML-KEM-768 key pair are generated on the device and kept in its secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore-backed storage on Android). The private halves never leave it.

  2. Only public keys are shared

    Your contact's app combines a classic X25519 exchange with an ML-KEM-768 encapsulation. Both secrets are mixed together, so the chat key holds even if one of the two methods is ever broken — including by a future quantum computer.

  3. Every message is sealed

    Messages and files are encrypted with XSalsa20-Poly1305. Any change made in transit is detected and the message is rejected.

  4. You verify, not us

    Compare the safety number in person or on a call. If someone's key changes, Televo shows a warning and won't send until you decide.

Calls that ring like a phone call.

Voice and video calls use your phone's own calling screen, so they ring even when Televo is closed. Audio and video are encrypted between the two phones. When a direct connection isn't possible, a relay passes the encrypted stream along without being able to decrypt it.

Where the line is today.

What works now, what we're building, and the limits we think you should know about before you rely on us.

Works today

  • End-to-end encrypted Secret Chats with safety numbers
  • Regular chats encrypted on the server and synced to every device
  • 1:1 voice and video calls that ring with the app closed
  • Groups, voice messages, disappearing messages
  • Two-step verification, passcode lock, QR sign-in, device list
  • Public source code

Building next

  • Group calls
  • Message search and reactions
  • Forward secrecy for Secret Chats: rotating keys, so one stolen key can't unlock past messages
  • Noise suppression and audio routing in calls

Honest limits

  • Regular chats are readable by Televo if we had to. Use a Secret Chat for anything you wouldn't want a server to hold.
  • Even in Secret Chats we see metadata: who talks to whom and when.
  • Secret Chats are tied to the device. A reinstall means old Secret Chat messages can't be opened.
  • The code hasn't had an independent security audit yet.
