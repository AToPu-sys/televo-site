Most apps ask you to trust them. We just made it so you don't have to.

When you open a Secret Chat, your phone creates the keys that will lock it, right there in your hand. Those keys never travel to a server. A message you send is already sealed before it leaves your device, and only the person you're talking to can open it.

If someone asked us to hand over a Secret Chat, we'd have nothing readable to give them. Not a policy we follow — a fact about how the math works.

Technical details, for the curious
01

Device generates two keypairs

An X25519 keypair and an ML-KEM-768 (post-quantum) keypair are created locally the first time you open Televo and stored in the secure enclave — neither private half ever leaves the device.

02

Both public keys are exchanged

Only the public halves are published to your profile. Your contact's app combines a classic X25519 Diffie–Hellman handshake with an ML-KEM-768 encapsulation, so the conversation stays secure even against a future quantum computer, not just today's attackers.

03

You verify it yourself

Compare safety numbers in person or over another channel. A match means no one sat in the middle of that handshake.

Calls that just work, and stay private.
